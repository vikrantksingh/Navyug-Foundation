Women and Kid’s Kabaddi Fitness Match - April 2026

Kabaddi is not just a sport, it’s a fun way to stay fit, build confidence, and connect with other women.

Navyug Foundation is organizing a fun Kabaddi match for women and kids to promote fitness, confidence, and an active lifestyle. It’s a great opportunity to enjoy sports, stay healthy, and be part of a positive community.

Unleash Your Inner Champion: Join the Navyug Kabaddi Fitness Festival!

Are you ready for a day of power, agility, and pure joy? The Navyug Foundation is thrilled to announce our first-ever Women and Kid’s Kabaddi Fitness Match!

We are taking this iconic Indian sport, traditionally known for strength and stamina, and reimagining it as a fun, accessible, and high-energy fitness celebration for women of all ages and children.

Forget the dust and intensity of professional leagues—this is about laughter, community, and proving that fitness knows no age or gender.

Why Kabaddi?

Kabaddi is more than just a game; it is an incredible full-body workout. Participating in our fitness match is the perfect way to:

  • Boost Stamina & Strength: The short bursts of running and grappling are fantastic for cardiovascular health and muscle building.
  • Enhance Agility & Coordination: Maneuvering on the court requires quick thinking and sharp reflexes.
  • Build Confidence: There is nothing quite like the rush of a successful raid or defense!
  • Empowerment: This is a celebration of women and children taking charge of their health and happiness through sport.

It's Your Turn to Raid!

Do not miss this chance to be part of something groundbreaking for the Navyug Foundation. Whether you are a mom wanting to lead by example, a grandmother looking for a fun activity, or a child eager to play, this event is for YOU.
