About

Bijay Singh, the esteemed Founder and Vice President of Navyug Foundation since 2015, is a dedicated advocate for social service and community development. A graduate of Darbhanga University, he brings over 42 years of experience in the fields of health, security, and disaster management. His unwavering commitment to social justice has made him a beacon of hope for communities in Bihar and beyond.

Bijay Singh has been instrumental in leading initiatives that address critical issues such as healthcare access, disaster preparedness, and community security. Through Navyug Foundation, he has mobilized a team to deliver impactful programs that uplift marginalized groups and foster sustainable development.

A passionate orator, Bijay Singh has delivered numerous speeches emphasizing the importance of equality, empowerment, and resilience. His words inspire action and cultivate awareness among diverse audiences, making him a trusted voice for change.

Under his leadership, Navyug Foundation has become a vital force in Bihar, championing social welfare and advocating for justice. Bijay Singh’s lifelong dedication to improving lives reflects his deep-seated values and vision for a more equitable society. His relentless efforts continue to leave a lasting impact on individuals and communities alike.
