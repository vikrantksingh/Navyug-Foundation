What We Do Globally


It is extremely important for you to know what we do with the help of donors; Donors, who are people like you, or are leading organizations or International bodies, all come together to help us do what we do.

Healthcare Services

Fulfill the basic health and social needs of underprivileged sections of the society like poor, women, elderly, children etc.

Education for Children

Engage to impart completely free education to the children of Economically Weaker Sections of the society.

Women Empowerment

The empowerment and autonomy of women and the improvement of their political, social, economic and health status is a highly important end in itself.

Disaster Management

Collaborating with government agencies and other bodies in different levels to develop capacity and skills to handle and mitigate the effects of disasters.

Social Services

Pursuing activities to relieve suffering, promote the interests of the poor, protect the environment, provide basic social services, or undertake community development.

Protecting Our Environment

Taking proactive steps to help, protect and heal natural areas like Challenges of pollution, global warming, natural resource depletion, and waste disposal.
